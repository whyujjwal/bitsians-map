# BITSians Startup Map — design spec

## v4 — "Field map" (2026-11-09, supersedes v3's dark "City lights" theme)

Light, map-first layout with floating cards, modelled on map-based job boards. Tokens live in `src/styles/global.css` (`--paper #E9ECE8`, `--surface #FFF`, `--ink #16201B`, `--peacock #0A6C74`, `--hire #23863F`; all text pairs ≥ 4.5:1).

- **Frame:** desktop is an 80px icon rail (Discover, Saved, Applied, About) beside a rounded stage (28px) holding a full-bleed map.
- **Base maps:** Esri's keyless raster services, switchable in the map's top-right control: **Map** (World Street Map) and **Satellite** (World Imagery plus the boundaries-and-places label overlay). The choice is remembered per browser. Glyphs for our own labels are self-hosted in `public/map/fonts`, so no other map host is needed; if base tiles fail three times the map says so and offers "Try again". (OpenFreeMap failed to load for some visitors; CARTO now watermarks keyless tiles.)
- **Floating cards over the map:** top bar (wordmark, search, Saved n, Applied n, "Add a company"); list card left (380px); detail card right (420px) with a green "Hiring now" banner, icon fact rows, Open roles card (careers CTA + tracker), founders, sources. Bottom-left pills: company count, hiring toggle, not-on-the-map.
- **Map:** no campus-to-city arcs. Campuses are the strongest marker: a 32px peacock pin with a cap icon and an always-visible label with the alumni-company count. Cities below z9 are dots (green = someone hiring) with white label pills, placed on whichever side is free (right, left, below, above) and never off-screen; label priority is cities with 10+ companies, then campuses, then the rest. From z9, logo chips with a green ring when hiring, names under them from z10.
- **Location filter:** one "Location" menu with Continent, Country, State and City groups (states from `src/lib/regions.ts`); changing any place filter refits the map to the results. The camera's padding follows the cards (`inset` prop on Map) and is only changed when the camera is still.
- **Mobile:** the map fills the screen; top bar is logo + search + About; bottom pills are List/Map, hiring, Saved, Applied; list is a full card, detail a bottom sheet.
- **Green means hiring** (replaces marigold). Black is the primary button colour.

## v3.1 — polish pass (2026-11-09)

From `ux-audit-site-2026-11-09.md`. Supersedes v3/v2 where they conflict.

- **Batch tag**, the one signature detail: wherever a founder appears (list row, detail, company page) the campus and batch read `Pilani ’08`, or `Class of ’10` when only the year is known, in `--ink-3` with tabular figures and a `title` spelling it out. A bare year is never shown.
- **One accent per row:** stage labels are always `--ink-2`; marigold is the only coloured label (Hiring). Peacock stays for selection, links and focus.
- **Map labels:** Pilani campus is labelled first, then cities with 3+ companies, then the other campuses, then small cities. Markers more than 75° from the view centre (past the globe horizon) are hidden.
- **Unmapped companies:** the off-map notice is a button ("146 aren’t on the map yet. Show them") that turns on the `unmapped` filter (`?unmapped=1`) and opens the list; the filter shows as a removable "Not on the map" chip.
- **Trust:** the detail panel lists sources by domain; detail and company page end with a quiet "Suggest a fix" link (prefilled GitHub issue); About links "Add a company".
- **Tokens and rhythm:** `--ink-3` is `#7A8E97` (4.5:1 on `--surface`). Spacing sits on a 4px grid. Chrome text (chips, counts, buttons, links) uses 500, emphasis 600; the wordmark, display headline and company names keep their v2 weights. Mobile tap targets are 44px (chips and text buttons get an invisible hit area).

> **v3 "City lights" (below, first) supersedes v2 wherever they conflict.** v2 still defines the panel, list rows, filters, detail view, mobile layout and company page.

## v3 — City lights

The owner wants the site to feel jaw-dropping on first sight. Concept: **the world at night, lit by the companies BITSians started; the four campuses are where the light comes from.** Keep every interaction instant: all effects run on the GPU (MapLibre layers), data stays inline, no new runtime requests except map tiles and logo images.

### Theme

- **Dark only.** The whole site uses the dark tokens from v2 (set `color-scheme: dark`; drop the light-mode switching). Page background `--paper: #0B141A` (a touch deeper than v2's dark paper so the map and chrome merge).
- The map is always the night style below, on every page state.

### Night map style (`public/map/night.json`, replaces light.json/dark.json)

Generated by `scripts/build-map-styles.mjs` from OpenFreeMap `dark`: no raster source, minimal layers.

| Element | Value |
|---|---|
| space / ocean | `#060C11` |
| land | `#0E1A22` |
| country borders | `#21384A`, 0.6px |
| state borders (z4+) | `#172A36`, 0.5px, dashed |
| major roads (motorway, trunk, primary; z7+) | `rgba(241,161,10,0.28)` with `line-blur: 1`, width interpolating 0.4px at z7 to 2px at z13 — streets glow faintly amber, like a city seen from orbit |
| place labels | country `#3B5566` 11px; city/town (z6+) `#5E7A89` 12px; halo `#0E1A22` |
| projection | `globe` (MapLibre 6), auto-flattens to mercator as you zoom in |
| sky/atmosphere | atmosphere halo tinted `#1E6A72` fading to transparent, `atmosphere-blend` high at low zoom, 0 by z6 |

### Map layers (bottom to top)

1. **Arcs** — great-circle LineStrings (48 points each) from a campus to each city where alumni of that campus founded companies (only founders with a known campus; aggregate per campus–city pair, weight = number of companies). `lineMetrics: true`, `line-gradient` from `#44B3B0` (campus end) to `#F1A10A` (city end), width `0.8 + 0.6·log2(weight)` px, opacity 0.5. Hidden at z ≥ 7.
2. **City lights** (z < 9) — per city group (v2 grouping): an outer glow circle (`circle-blur: 1`, radius `2.6·r`, colour `#F1A10A` @ 0.22 if any company there is hiring, else `#44B3B0` @ 0.18) and a core circle (radius `r = 3 + 2.2·sqrt(count)` px, colour `#FFE3A3` if hiring else `#BDF2EC`, `circle-blur: 0.25`). City name + count as DOM labels (Archivo, v2 city-label style) from z3; at z < 3 only the five biggest cities are labelled.
3. **Campuses** — four fixed points: Pilani `[75.5870, 28.3639]`, Goa `[73.8782, 15.3911]`, Hyderabad `[78.5718, 17.5449]`, Dubai `[55.4196, 25.1310]`. A thin white ring (8px, 1.5px stroke) with a DOM label "Pilani campus" etc. in `--ink-2`. Clicking one filters by that campus (App does the filtering).
4. **Logo sunflowers** (z ≥ 9) — v2 sunflower positions. Each tile is a rounded square chip: white (`#FFFFFF`) behind normal logos, `#13222C` behind logos flagged `logoDark`, marigold ring when hiring, 45% opacity when inactive. The logo image (`/logos/<file>.png`, loaded on demand through the `styleimagemissing` event, cached with `map.addImage`) sits on the chip; companies without a logo keep the v2 monogram tile. Selected/hovered: peacock ring via feature-state.

### The one orchestrated moment (first load only)

1. t = 0: hero headline is already in the HTML (server-rendered), over a `#060C11` placeholder, so first paint is instant and never blank.
2. When the style loads: the globe sits tilted toward India and rotates slowly eastward (about 4°/s) until India is centred (about 2 s), then stops.
3. 150–1400 ms: city lights fade up, biggest first (stagger by rank, opacity via feature-state with paint transitions).
4. 500–1900 ms: arcs draw outward from the campuses (animate the `line-gradient` stop / trim from campus to city).
5. Any interaction (drag, wheel, click, key, typing in search) ends the sequence instantly at its final frame. `prefers-reduced-motion`: render the final frame directly, no rotation.

**Bloom (user-triggered):** when the view crosses into z ≥ 9 over a city, that city's tiles fade in along the spiral (feature-state opacity, index-staggered, ≤ 450 ms total).

### Hero overlay (App, DOM over the map)

Bottom-left of the map on desktop, inside 16px gutters on mobile (above the List pill):

- Headline: "Built by BITSians." — Archivo `wdth` 62, `wght` 860, 92px desktop / 46px mobile, line-height 0.9, `--ink`. No coloured or italic word.
- Line under it (17px, `--ink-2`, max 46ch): "306 companies started by alumni of Pilani, Goa, Hyderabad and Dubai. 61 of them are hiring." Numbers are live (`tabular-nums`); "61 of them are hiring" is a button that turns on the Hiring filter.
- It collapses (200 ms fade/slide) the moment the user filters, searches, selects a company or interacts with the map (Map calls `oninteract()` once), and does not come back until reload.

### Logos everywhere

- List rows, detail header and company pages show the logo on its chip (36 / 48 / 64 px) with `loading="lazy" decoding="async"` and explicit width/height; fallback is the monogram tile.
- Data fields (added by `scripts/export_web_data.py`): `logo` (e.g. `/logos/swiggy.png`, or `""`), `logoDark` (boolean: logo is light-on-transparent and needs the dark chip).

### Map ↔ App contract additions

- New optional prop on Map: `oninteract?: () => void` (fired once on the first user interaction with the map).
- `Prepared` gains `logo` and `logoDark`.

# v2 (base spec)

Subject: a map and directory of companies founded by BITS Pilani alumni (all four campuses).
Audience: BITSians — students hunting internships and jobs, alumni networking, founders hiring.
Primary job: show where BITSian companies are, who founded them (campus, batch), and get people to their open roles fast.

## Principles

1. **Hiring is the loudest thing on screen.** Marigold means "hiring now" and nothing else uses it.
2. **One visual unit everywhere: the monogram tile.** The same rounded-square tile with 1–2 initials is the brand mark, the map pin and the list avatar.
3. **The map is honest about precision.** Almost every location is city-level, so companies are shown as a city bubble when zoomed out and as a sunflower of tiles around the city centre when zoomed in — never as fake street-level pins. Exact (address-level) points sit at their real location.
4. **Dense and calm.** Rows separated by hairlines, not floating cards with shadows. Spend boldness on the map and the tiles only.
5. **Motion answers actions only:** fly-to on select, sheet slide on mobile. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (jump instead of fly).

Avoid (generic-AI tells): all-caps labels or eyebrows above headings, meta strings joined with middle dots (" · "), "Word — fragment" labels, monospace data labels, "→" appended to links/buttons, identical rounded cards with the same grey shadow, gradient washes, a rainbow categorical palette on the map.

## Tokens

Define on `:root`, redefine for dark under `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)`.

| Token | Light | Dark | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--paper` | `#F4F6F3` | `#0E1920` | page background |
| `--surface` | `#FFFFFF` | `#13212A` | panels, sheets |
| `--ink` | `#13222C` | `#E7EEF0` | primary text, non-hiring tiles |
| `--ink-2` | `#4B5D68` | `#A3B4BB` | secondary text |
| `--ink-3` | `#7C8C95` | `#6E828B` | tertiary text, placeholders |
| `--line` | `#DCE3E0` | `#233541` | hairlines, borders |
| `--peacock` | `#0A6C74` | `#44B3B0` | primary: selection, city bubbles, links, focus ring |
| `--on-peacock` | `#FFFFFF` | `#0E1920` | text on peacock |
| `--marigold` | `#F1A10A` | `#F4B02E` | hiring only |
| `--on-marigold` | `#13222C` | `#13222C` | text on marigold |

Radii: tiles 6px (small) / 9px (large); pills/chips 999px; panels and sheets 0 on desktop, 16px top corners on mobile sheets. Shadows: only on floating map overlays (`0 1px 2px rgb(0 0 0 / .08), 0 4px 16px rgb(0 0 0 / .08)`), never on list rows.
Focus: `outline: 2px solid var(--peacock); outline-offset: 2px` on every interactive element.

One family: **Archivo** (variable, `wght` + `wdth` axes), self-hosted via `@fontsource-variable/archivo` (latin subset only), `font-display: swap`, preload the latin woff2. Fallback stack: `"Archivo Variable", "Archivo", system-ui, sans-serif`.

| Detail title (company name) | 30px / 1.05 | 75 | 780 |

| City label (map) | 15px / 1 | 70 | 760 |

## Components

**Monogram tile** — rounded square, initials from the company name (existing `initials()`), centred.
- Hiring (has careers link): fill `--marigold`, text `--on-marigold`.
- Active, not known hiring: fill `--ink`, text `--paper`.
- Acquired or closed (`status` starts with "acquired" or equals "closed"): transparent fill, 1.5px `--ink-3` border, text `--ink-3`.
- Size: 28px for Unicorn / Soonicorn / Minicorn / Public, 22px otherwise (map); 36px in list rows; 48px in detail.
- Selected: 2px `--peacock` ring outside the tile with 2px gap.

**List row** — tile, name (row title style), stage label right-aligned (plain text in `--ink-2`; Unicorn/Soonicorn/Minicorn get `--peacock` text), one-liner (2 lines max, clamp), founders line: names, plus campus and year as "Pilani 2008" in `--ink-3`, city in `--ink-3`. If hiring: a small marigold "Hiring" pill. Whole row is one button (selects); hover/focus background `color-mix(in oklab, var(--peacock) 7%, transparent)`; hovering a row highlights its tile on the map. Rows use `content-visibility: auto; contain-intrinsic-size: 96px`.

**Filter chips (desktop)** — pill buttons: "Hiring 61" toggle (marigold when on), then "Sector", "Stage", "Campus", "City" dropdowns. A dropdown chip shows its active count ("Sector 2") and is filled `--ink` / text `--paper` when active. Its popover is a checkbox list with counts, keyboard navigable, closes on Esc / outside click. Sector order: by count, but "Other" always last. "Closed" stage last. "Clear" text button appears only when something is active.

**Sort** — "Notable" (stage weight, then name — current order), "Newest" (founded desc, unknown last), "A–Z".

**Result count** — "306 companies" plus "61 hiring" in marigold text, `aria-live="polite"`.

**Empty state** — "No companies match these filters." + "Clear filters" button.

**Not on map** — when some filtered companies lack coordinates, a small overlay at the map's bottom-left: "114 of these aren't on the map yet" (it stays honest; the list has them all).

## Map

**Styles** — own light and dark MapLibre styles served from `/map/light.json` and `/map/dark.json` (same origin, cached), generated once by `web/scripts/build-map-styles.mjs` from OpenFreeMap's `positron` (light) and `dark` styles and committed to `public/map/`. The script: drops the `ne2_shaded` raster source and its layers; drops POI, building, house-number, aeroway, transit and minor-road layers; keeps background, water, landcover/park, major roads (motorway, trunk, primary) from z8, country and state boundaries, and place labels (country, state at z4+, city, town at z8+); recolours with the palette below; keeps OpenFreeMap's `glyphs` and `sprite` URLs and the `openmaptiles` vector source. Switch style when the colour scheme changes (re-add custom sources/layers after `style.load`).

| Map element | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|
| land / background | `#EDF1EE` | `#12202A` |
| water | `#C5D6DD` | `#0A141B` |
| park / landcover | `#E1E9DD` | `#14262B` |
| major roads | `#FFFFFF` (casing `#DCE3E0`) | `#1E303B` |
| boundaries | `#AAB7BD` | `#2E4350` |
| place labels | `#50616B`, halo `#EDF1EE` | `#8FA3AD`, halo `#12202A` |

**Two representations by zoom (no GL clustering):**
- **z < 9: city bubbles.** Group the filtered companies by `city + country`. One bubble per group at the mean of the group's real coordinates. Circle fill `--peacock`, radius `8 + 4·sqrt(count)` px, count inside in `--on-peacock`; the city name sits to the right in the city-label type, and if any company there is hiring, "N hiring" in marigold under the name. Render the ~40 bubbles as DOM markers (so they use Archivo). Click a bubble: fly to the city at z11.
- **z ≥ 9: sunflower tiles.** Within each city group, order companies by stage weight then name, and place company *i* (0-based) at the city centre plus a golden-angle spiral offset: `angle = i · 137.508°`, `radius = s · sqrt(i + 0.5)` with `s = 1.9 km`, converting km to degrees with the latitude correction (`dLat = km / 110.574`, `dLng = km / (111.320 · cos(lat))`). Unicorns land in the middle. Address-level companies (`precision === "address"`) use their real coordinates instead. Draw tiles with GL layers (fast): a GeoJSON source with `promoteId: "id"`, a symbol layer using an SDF rounded-square icon created with `map.addImage(..., { sdf: true })` coloured per tile kind via `icon-color`, a ring icon for selected/hovered via feature-state, and the initials as `text-field` (OpenFreeMap glyphs, "Noto Sans Bold"). Allow overlap; keep 60fps panning.
- Below the tiles at z ≥ 9, show a quiet line in the map overlay: "Companies are grouped around each city centre; exact offices aren't mapped yet."

**Camera** — first load: if most filtered companies are in India, fit India (`[68, 6.5]` to `[97.5, 35.5]`), otherwise fit all. A small segmented control top-left: "India" / "World". Selecting a company: fly to its tile at z12 (jump if reduced motion). Filter changes do not move the camera unless the result set has no visible bubble/tile in view, then fit the results.

**Interaction contract** between `App.svelte` and `Map.svelte`:
- props: `items` (filtered `Prepared[]`), `all`, `selectedId: string`, `hoveredId: string`, `onselect(id: string)`.
- Map never owns filter state. App owns selection (`?c=<id>` in the URL; keep accepting the old `?focus=`).
